Four clusters injected one at a time at 250 pc into field 102 (r_half 2 pc), shown on the sky. Blue: injected members the detector put in the matched group; violet rings: injected members it missed; aqua: real field stars that joined the group. Titles give the surviving member count, the Jaccard overlap with the injected members and the group's CST (detected means CST > 3). Limitation: single draws illustrate the method; detection rates come from the main run (figure 5).
