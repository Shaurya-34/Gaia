How small a group can this search find?

The fitted detectability model from a Gaia DR3 injection-recovery test in one sky region (block 6). Move the sliders to see the probability that the baseline detector finds a co-moving group (CST > 3).

-

Valid only inside the fitted range: block 6's four fields, 50-500 pc, 5-2,000 surviving members, the PREREG quality cuts, Plummer clusters with twin-star photometry. Parallax error and local density are held at the block-6 medians (0.0518 mas, rho = 3.7844). Model: P = 1 / (1 + exp(-(log10 n - a) / sigma)), a = a0 + a1 d/100 + a2 log10(parallax error) + a3 log10(rho). Fit on 110 trials; checked on 36 held-out trials, not on unseen sky.
