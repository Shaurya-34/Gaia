Colour-magnitude diagrams (absolute G from the corrected parallax, no extinction correction). The Pleiades and alpha Per members recovered by the baseline trace tight single-age sequences; the injected cluster (right) does not, because each injected member copies the magnitude and colour of a random nearby real 'twin' star. Limitation: injected clusters have field-like CMDs, so CMD tightness is not used to train the vetter and survival against distance is not realistic.
